# Wallpaper Motivator — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: February 23, 2026**

Wallpaper Motivator does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

All settings and quote libraries are stored locally on your device in `~/Library/Application Support/WallpaperMotivator/` and are never sent to any server or third party.

The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting services, or any network connections of any kind.
